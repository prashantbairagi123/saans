# SAANS – Every breath, decoded. Even offline.

**Live website:** https://prashantbairagi123.github.io/saans/ · **App:** https://prashantbairagi123.github.io/saans/app.html

SAANS (साँस, *breath*) is a 146 KB offline air-pollution control app for India, in English and हिंदी.

## What's new in version 2
- **Ask SAANS:** an offline deep search in Hindi, English or Hinglish. It reads numbers in your question (AQI, PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, ozone, visibility, room size), runs the official formulas, shows every step, and adds the best answer from a checked library of 44 facts. Spelling mistakes are forgiven.
- **"Can I go out?" verdict:** Go / Go with care / Stay in, for the person (child, older adult, asthma, pregnancy, outdoor worker) and the activity, with a cleaner time window.
- **Listen:** advice and answers are read aloud with the phone's own voices.
- **Easier:** a 30-second first-run tour, a larger-text mode (A+), and a six-tab layout.

## Seven tools
Ask · Air (CPCB National AQI engine) · Sky-eye (visibility → PM2.5) · Detect (source detective + offline complaint) · Control (city policy lab) · Plan (cleanest hours, inhaled dose, purifier size) · Act (pledges, streaks, myths).

## Files
`index.html` (website) · `app.html` (the whole app in one file) · `sw.js`, `manifest.json`, icons (offline install) · `img/` (screenshots).

## Honest limits
Sky-eye and the source detective are estimates, not monitors. Source mixes and the daily curve are illustrative. Health tips are general guidance, not medical advice.
